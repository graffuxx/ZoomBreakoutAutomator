Support

Getting Help

Email contact@taectra.com for support.

Support hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 (Central European Time), excluding public holidays in Brandenburg, Germany. We aim to provide an initial response within two business days.

Before Contacting Support

Please include your Zoom desktop client version, whether you are host or co-host, and the exact error message shown by the app.

Common Checks

  • Use Zoom Desktop 7.0.5 or later.
  • Open the app from inside a Zoom Meeting.
  • Use the app as meeting host or co-host.
  • Make sure breakout rooms are available for the meeting.

Privacy And Legal Requests

Privacy, data-protection, and legal questions can also be sent to contact@taectra.com.
