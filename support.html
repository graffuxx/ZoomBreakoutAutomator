Zoom Breakout Automator

Support

Getting Help

For support, contact the developer through the support contact listed in the Zoom Marketplace app listing.

Before Contacting Support

Please include your Zoom desktop client version, whether you are host or co-host, and the exact error message shown by the app.

Common Checks

  • Use Zoom Desktop 7.0.5 or later.
  • Open the app from inside a Zoom Meeting.
  • Use the app as meeting host or co-host.
  • Make sure breakout rooms are available for the meeting.
