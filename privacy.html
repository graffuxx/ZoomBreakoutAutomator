Breakout Room Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Breakout Room Manager is an app for Zoom Meetings that helps meeting hosts and co-hosts create, rename, open, and close breakout rooms.

The app is provided by Jan Ritter. A Cloudflare Worker serves the public website files; the app has no application user-account backend or external database. The app does not sell personal information.

Data We Process

The app may access Zoom meeting context needed to run inside the Zoom client and manage breakout rooms when the user is host or co-host. Room presets and room names entered by the user are stored locally in the user's browser storage on the device.

The app code does not upload these presets or store Zoom OAuth tokens in a developer-operated database. User role and room information returned by the Zoom Apps SDK are used in memory to display and perform supported meeting actions. Local presets and preferences use browser storage without additional application-level encryption.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only to provide the app's core functionality: saving room presets locally and sending supported Zoom Apps SDK commands to manage breakout rooms in the active meeting.

Sharing And Retention

When you create or rename rooms, the selected room names and commands are sent to Zoom through the Zoom Apps SDK. Zoom processes meeting information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cloudflare hosts and delivers the public app files and processes connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata for delivery and security. This is separate from the room presets stored locally. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for information about its processing.

Locally saved room presets remain until you edit them or clear the site's app/browser storage. Removing authorization in Zoom is not a guarantee that local storage is deleted. The app code does not add analytics trackers.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, and to contact your data protection authority. You can edit your saved room names in the app. Clear this site's app/browser storage to remove locally saved presets and preferences; revoke the app in Zoom to remove its authorization.

For privacy requests, contact the developer through the support contact listed in the Zoom Marketplace app listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed in the Zoom Marketplace app listing.
