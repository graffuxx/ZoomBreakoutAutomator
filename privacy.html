Zoom Breakout Automator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Overview

Zoom Breakout Automator is a Zoom App for meeting hosts and co-hosts to create, rename, open, and close breakout rooms from inside Zoom Meetings.

The app does not operate an external backend server and does not sell personal information.

Data We Process

The app may access Zoom meeting context needed to run inside the Zoom client and manage breakout rooms when the user is host or co-host. Room presets and room names entered by the user are stored locally in the user's browser storage on the device.

The app does not store Zoom user data, meeting data, participant data, or room presets on an external server.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only to provide the app's core functionality: saving room presets locally and sending supported Zoom Apps SDK commands to manage breakout rooms in the active meeting.

Sharing And Retention

The app does not share personal information with third parties. Locally saved room presets remain on the user's device until the user clears browser/app storage or removes the app.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information. Because the app does not store data on an external server, most app data can be removed by clearing local app/browser storage or removing the app from Zoom.

For privacy requests, contact the developer through the support contact listed in the Zoom Marketplace app listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed in the Zoom Marketplace app listing.
