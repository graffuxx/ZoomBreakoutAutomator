# Anfrage an Zoom zur externen Abrechnung

Subject: Request for guidance on monetizing Breakout Room Manager as an EU developer

Hello Zoom Marketplace Team,

our Zoom App **Breakout Room Manager** is published and currently offered free of charge. We are developing an optional Pro plan while keeping the existing free functionality available.

The developer and payment recipient is a registered business located in Germany. Zoom's current Marketplace monetization documentation states that native Marketplace payment accounts are limited to businesses or individuals located in the United States. Therefore, we cannot complete the native Stripe payment-account onboarding with truthful business information.

Could you please confirm whether we may use our own EU-based Stripe account for an external subscription flow linked from the Zoom App?

The proposed setup would:

- keep a fully functional free plan in the Zoom Marketplace;
- clearly identify optional Pro features before purchase;
- open the checkout only in the user's system browser;
- process payments through our German Stripe business account;
- verify subscriptions server-side before enabling Pro features;
- provide cancellation, invoices, privacy information and support through TAECTRA;
- avoid storing payment-card data in the Zoom App or on our own servers.

If external billing is permitted, please let us know which Marketplace settings, disclosures, scopes or review materials you require before implementation and submission.
