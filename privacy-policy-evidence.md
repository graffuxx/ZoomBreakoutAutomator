# Privacy Policy Evidence

App: Zoom Breakout Automator  
Last updated: September 7, 2026

## Public Privacy Policy URL

The app's Privacy Policy is published at:

https://zoombreakoutautomator.graffuxxx.workers.dev/privacy.html

## Privacy Summary

The Privacy Policy explains that Zoom Breakout Automator:

- Does not sell personal information.
- Does not operate an external backend server.
- Does not store Zoom user data, meeting data, participant data, or OAuth tokens on an external server.
- Stores room presets locally in the user's browser storage only.
- Provides information about user data rights and how to contact the developer.

## Data Subject Rights

The policy informs users that, depending on location, they may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information. Because the app does not store user data on an external server, users can remove locally stored app data by clearing local browser/app storage or removing the app from Zoom.
